Metro Track
פסי מטרו
Cost 40 cr per tileעלות 40 למשבצת
Unlocks at 1200 popנפתח ב-1200 תושבים
Underground view. Drag to lay metro track (40 cr per tile, 0.3 upkeep). Link Metro Stations with track to cut congestion.
Metro Station
תחנת מטרו
Cost 2500 cr per tileעלות 2500 למשבצת
Unlocks at 1200 popנפתח ב-1200 תושבים
Works in the surface or underground view. Tap free land or a surface Station tile (2,500 cr, 15 upkeep). Link it by metro track to a station that touches the tubes and it lets neighbouring zones grow.
Remove Metro
הסרת מטרו
Underground view. Tap or drag to remove only metro track or stations. The surface above is never touched.
