## Done in v1.3
- Zones merge 2x2 for housing, shops, factories and tourism; zones two tiles from a tube now count as connected.
- Tourism works without a Spaceport (Spaceport gives 1.7x income). Tourist income raised.
- Rotate button for non-square buildings (Safety Station, Emergency Response, Battery Bank).
- Map is 48x48 (old 32x32 cities are moved into the bigger map).
- Water and ice: ice outpost 25->70, plant 30->90, per-resident need 0.08->0.05.
- Warning button replaces the top banners; opens a list of what is missing.
- Regolith is built in: homes come with shielding, no separate tool.
- Free landmarks at population milestones 100, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000 (Sights tab).
- New terrain: basalt, highland dust, boulder fields, mountains; more ice deposits.
- Level terrain tool: flatten craters (150/tile) and mountains (400/tile).
